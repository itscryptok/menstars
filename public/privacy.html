Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. What we collect

Account data: your email and a securely hashed password. Profile data you provide: display name, claimed social handles, niches, brand info. Content you create: engager lists, stars, and private notes. Technical data: basic logs needed to keep the service running.

2. What is public

Engager handles, star counts, leaderboard rankings, claimed handles, and "open to brand deals" status are public — that is the point of the network.

3. What is always private

Your private engager notes and your private notepad are visible only to you. They are never shown to other users, never sold, never used for advertising, and never included in any report or export. Even our admin tools are built to never read them.

4. What we never do

We never sell your data. We never sell stars, ranking, or leaderboard placement — ranking cannot be bought, period. We never share your email with third parties for marketing.

5. Cookies & local storage

We use a login session cookie and local browser storage for preferences (theme, dismissed banners). No third-party tracking cookies.

6. Your control

You can edit or delete your engagers, notes, and profile at any time. Deleting your account removes your data.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: Cryptok Solutions.
